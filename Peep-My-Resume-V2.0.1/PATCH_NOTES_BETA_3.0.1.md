# Peep-My-Resume Beta 3.0.1

- Parse Check shows a **What to do** suggestion and a link to the relevant results for each review finding. The green, yellow, and red ratings remain.
- Experience distinguishes unassigned text, incomplete role fields, and an employer overview whose roles were detected separately. The overview card now says **Employer overview**.
- Education and Other / Unclassified findings explain what to compare and when changing the formatting may help. Link, contact, and text extraction findings include their own next steps.
- The API adds `parseCheck.findings` alongside the existing reasons, and `/health` reports `3.0.1-beta`.
